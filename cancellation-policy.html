Cancellation Policy

SREE DEV HOSPITALITY · Legal Name as per GST: SREE DEV

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Requesting cancellation

To request cancellation, email support@sreedev.in or call +91 9840238910 with your order or quotation reference, contact details and reason. A request is effective only after we confirm its status.

Before order acceptance

Enquiries and draft quotations can be withdrawn before order acceptance, subject to any separately authorized work or charges already agreed.

After order acceptance

For confirmed orders, cancellation depends on whether stock has been allocated, goods procured or dispatched, customization started, installation scheduled, or services activated. We will explain any applicable charges or recoverable balance based on documented costs and agreed terms, subject to law.

Customized goods and scheduled services

Custom-printed or branded hotel supplies and special procurement may not be cancellable without costs once production or purchasing begins. Hotel technology installations, subscriptions and consulting assignments may have separate milestone or provider-specific cancellation conditions communicated before confirmation.

Delays or inability to fulfill

If we cannot fulfill an accepted order, we will contact you to discuss a revised schedule, suitable alternative or cancellation and any refund due under applicable law and the agreed terms.

Cancellation and refunds

Any approved refund resulting from a cancellation is handled under our Refund Policy. Payment gateway or bank processing time can vary.
