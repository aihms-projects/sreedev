Refund Policy

SREE DEV HOSPITALITY · Legal Name as per GST: SREE DEV

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Scope

This policy explains how refund requests are considered for orders and services purchased from SREE DEV (as per GST), operating as SREE DEV HOSPITALITY. Specific written quotations, order confirmations or agreements may contain additional lawful terms.

Eligibility assessment

Refund eligibility depends on the product or service, order stage, whether goods were dispatched or customized, work already performed, supplier commitments and applicable law. A payment alone does not establish automatic refund eligibility. Statutory rights remain unaffected.

Standard stocked products

For eligible standard products, contact us promptly with the invoice or order reference, reason and supporting photos where relevant. We will review whether cancellation, return, replacement or refund is appropriate. Do not return goods without receiving return instructions.

Customized goods and services

Logo-branded amenities, made-to-order products, installed hardware, activated subscriptions and completed consulting or implementation work may incur non-recoverable costs. Any applicable deductions or non-refundable components should be disclosed in the written order terms, subject to law.

Incorrect, damaged or defective items

If goods arrive damaged, incorrect or defective, notify us as soon as reasonably possible with the invoice and evidence. We will assess replacement, repair, return or refund options as required by applicable law and the agreed warranty.

Approved refund method and timeline

Once a refund is approved, we will communicate the approved amount, method and expected processing timeframe. Refunds are generally initiated to the original payment method where feasible; bank or payment gateway settlement times may vary. No fixed processing period is promised unless confirmed for the specific transaction.

How to request a refund

Email support@sreedev.in or call +91 9840238910 with the customer name, order or transaction ID, payment date, amount, reason and relevant supporting documents. Never share OTPs, PINs or full card details.
